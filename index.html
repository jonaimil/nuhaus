Should I Live in Japan?

You've thought about it. Maybe you've even dreamed about it. But there's a question you need to answer from experience, not imagination: Could I actually build a life here?

Nuhaus is where you find out. You stay for weeks, not days. You experience daily life. You meet people asking the same question. You test your assumptions and discover what's real. When you decide—yes or no—you'll know because you've lived it.

We Make Your Transition Real

We handle the overwhelming parts: visas, housing, employment, language, cultural navigation. We connect you with the right people and resources. We create community so you're never alone in the uncertainty.

But more importantly, we give you space to discover. Space to explore neighborhoods. Space to connect with collaborators. Space to figure out who you become when you build in Japan. That's the real work. We're here to support it.

Japan is Ready. Are You?

Japan needs international talent. The government is opening doors. Companies are recruiting. The creative community is hungry for fresh perspectives. The timing has never been better.

But beyond what Japan needs is what you need: people asking the same questions, a place to test your assumptions, a community ready to build with you. That's what we've created. The question is: are you ready to explore what's possible?
