Should I Live in Japan?

You've thought about it. Maybe you've even dreamed about it. But there's a question you need to answer from experience, not imagination: Could I build a life in Japan?

Nuhaus is where you find out. Stay for weeks, not days. Experience daily life. Meet people asking the same question. You test your assumptions and discover what's real. When you decide, it will be through lived experience.

How do I make the move?

We guide you with: visas, housing, employment, language and cultural navigation. Connecting you with the right people and resources, to community so you're never uncertain.

More importantly, we give you space to discover. Space to explore neighborhoods. Space to connect with collaborators. Space to figure out who you become when you build in Japan. That's the real work. We're here to support it.

Japan is Ready. Are You?

Japan needs international talent. The government is opening doors. Companies are recruiting. The creative community is hungry for fresh perspectives. The timing has never been better.

Beyond what Japan needs, is what you need: people asking the same questions, a place to test your assumptions, a community ready to build with you. That's what we've created. Are you ready to explore what's possible?
